# wisp 0.4.10

- Clean source selection: removed technical token and playback-routing subtitles from onboarding and settings.
- Added a persistent Butterfly checkbox in `/settings`. Turning it off hides the sprite and stops its animation; turning it back on resumes it.
- Refined Aurora and Afterglow's compact W gradients with color blended per block across both axes.
- Kept all settings reachable in smaller terminal windows.

## Update or install

```powershell
irm https://github.com/cmuty/wisp/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex
```

Close and reopen wisp after updating. Existing profiles keep the butterfly enabled
until you turn it off. Settings and collections are retained.
