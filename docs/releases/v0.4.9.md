# wisp 0.4.9

Theme setup now keeps the W logo compact across all five themes. Aurora and
Afterglow preserve their gradients and use Russian names when Russian is selected:
«Сияние» and «Послесвечение».

## Update or install

```powershell
irm https://github.com/cmuty/wisp/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex
```

Close and reopen wisp after updating. Your existing profile and collections are retained.
