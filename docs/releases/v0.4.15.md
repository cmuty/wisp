# wisp 0.4.15

## Bring your SoundCloud likes

Import a SoundCloud profile's public liked tracks directly into **Likes**:

```text
/import https://soundcloud.com/username/likes
```

- Browse and play imported tracks from the Likes section.
- Existing likes are preserved; duplicate recordings are skipped.
- Reimport the same profile to add newly discovered liked tracks. Removing likes on SoundCloud does not remove local likes.
- All pages must load successfully before anything is saved. No partial import is kept after a failed request.
- Imports do not modify the SoundCloud account. Private or unavailable tracks remain subject to source availability.

Update by closing wisp and rerunning the PowerShell installer:

```powershell
irm https://github.com/cmuty/wisp/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex
```

Scoop users can run `scoop update; scoop update wisp`. The EXE installer can also upgrade an existing installation. Your local profile and collections are preserved.
