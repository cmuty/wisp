# wisp 0.4.13

- The track list now marks the current recording with a play or pause indicator, independently of the selection cursor.
- Automatic queue transitions move the selection to the current recording. Pressing Enter pauses or resumes it instead of restarting the previously selected track.
- Browsing with arrow keys or the mouse wheel keeps your selection in place during automatic playback transitions. Enter still plays a deliberately selected recording.
- Enter also pauses the current recording when the queue has moved beyond the visible search results.

Existing profiles, playlists, and audio settings are preserved.
